## 1. r/SideProject (easiest, most forgiving — post here first)

**Title:** I built Kalium — type a sentence, get a finished image (50 free credits, no card)

**Body:**

Hey everyone — I've been building Kalium, an AI image generator that runs on
Google's nano banana model.

The pitch: you type a plain sentence, you get a finished image. No seeds, no
negative prompts, no parameter rabbit holes. It also does edits — drop in up to
14 reference photos and describe the change ("make the jacket leather, light it
at golden hour") and it keeps faces and products intact.

Some details I'm proud of:
- Every sample image on the landing page was generated by the product itself,
  and hovering shows the exact prompt that made it
- The whole thing feels a bit like a game — screen shake when your render
  lands, a command-bar UI instead of a settings form
- 50 free credits on sign-up, no card required. Pay-per-use after that, no
  subscription

Would love brutal feedback on the landing page and the generator UX.

## 2. Show HN (Hacker News — technical crowd, be modest and concrete)

**Title:** Show HN: Kalium – text-to-image app on Google's nano banana model

**Body:**

I built a web app around Google's nano banana image model (via fal.ai):
type a sentence, get an image; or upload up to 14 reference images and
describe an edit in plain language.

Stack: Next.js 16, Prisma/Postgres (Neon), NextAuth, Stripe, fal queue API
with webhook + polling fallback. The landing page's sample gallery is
generated by the product, with the real prompts shown on hover.

Free tier is 50 credits (≈4 renders), pay-per-use after, no subscription.

Happy to answer anything about the model's behaviour (typography is
surprisingly good, faces across edits mostly hold up) or the plumbing.
